# Noisometer — Support

Questions, problems or feature requests? Write to polsaleksa@gmail.com and I will reply.

**Readings look wrong.** iPhone microphones vary between models. Noisometer is pre-calibrated, but readings are estimates and become unreliable above roughly 100 dB.

**Location is not recorded.** Location access is optional. Enable it in iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Noisometer.
